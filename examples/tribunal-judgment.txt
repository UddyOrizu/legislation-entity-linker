In the Employment Tribunal
BETWEEN: A Claimant and B Respondent

1. The claimant brings claims under the Employment Rights Act 1996 ("the ERA")
   and the Equality Act 2010. She relies in particular on section 94 of the ERA
   and on sections 13, 19 and 26 of the Equality Act 2010.

2. The respondent contends that the transfer was governed by the Transfer of
   Undertakings (Protection of Employment) Regulations 2006 (SI 2006/246), and
   that regulation 4 of those Regulations operated to transfer liability. The
   claimant accepts that TUPE applied.

3. It is common ground that the respondent processed the claimant's personal
   data. The claimant relies on the Data Protection Act 2018 ("the DPA") and in
   particular on section 45(2)(a) of the DPA. The 2018 Act must be read
   compatibly with Convention rights: see section 3 of the Human Rights Act
   1998, and section 6 of that Act.

4. As to disclosure, the tribunal was referred to Part 31 of the Civil Procedure
   Rules 1998 and to paragraph 4 of Schedule 2 to the Companies Act 2006.

5. The respondent's health and safety obligations arise under the Health and
   Safety at Work etc. Act 1974, s 2, and under the Management of Health and
   Safety at Work Regulations 1999. COSHH is not engaged.

6. Finally, the claimant relies on the Public Interest Disclosure Act 1998,
   ss 1-3, and on the Protection from Harassment Act 1997.
